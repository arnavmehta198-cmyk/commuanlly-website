Questions

Frequently asked

Is Communally really free?

Yes! Communally is 100% free to download and use. We don't take any cut from your earnings. You keep everything you make.

How do I get paid?

Payment is handled directly between you and your neighbor. Most people use Venmo, Cash App, or cash. We don't process payments through the app.

Is it safe to meet strangers?

Every user is verified with their phone number and email. You can see ratings and reviews from other neighbors before connecting. Always meet in public places for first-time jobs.

What kind of jobs can I find?

Common jobs include dog walking, pet sitting, lawn care, moving help, cleaning, errands, tutoring, handyman tasks, and more. If a neighbor needs help, you'll see it.

Is there an Android version?

We're currently iOS only, but Android is coming soon! Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it launches.

What cities is Communally available in?

Communally works everywhere in the US. The more neighbors who join in your area, the more opportunities you'll see. Invite your neighbors to grow your local community!

A Note From Us

Why We Built Communally

"Growing up in Pleasanton, we noticed something: neighbors didn't really know each other anymore. People needed help with simple tasks, and others wanted to earn money — but there was no easy way to connect. We built Communally to change that. Our goal is simple: bring communities closer together, one connection at a time."

— Shaurya & Arnav
